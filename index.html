About Us

Steel Built for Bangladesh's Growth

Headquartered in Chattogram — the heart of Bangladesh's steel industry — J.M. Steel Industries Ltd is a manufacturer of long and flat steel products for construction, infrastructure, fabrication and industrial customers.

We convert steel scrap, billets and plates into finished products through our own melting furnaces, reheating furnaces and hot and cold rolling mills. Controlling each stage of production lets us hold tight tolerances, consistent mechanical properties and dependable delivery schedules for contractors, dealers, fabricators and project owners.

Manufacturing

From Molten Metal to Finished Steel

Our integrated process controls quality at every stage — raw material, melting, casting, rolling and finishing.

Raw Material Selection

Sorted melting scrap, sponge iron, billets and plates are inspected and segregated by grade and chemistry before charging.

Melting & Refining

Induction / electric melting furnaces melt the charge; ladle refining adjusts carbon, manganese and alloy content to the target grade.

Continuous Casting

Molten steel is cast into square billets through a water-cooled mould, producing a sound, uniform internal structure.

Reheating

Billets are reheated to rolling temperature (around 1,100–1,200 °C) in a reheating furnace for uniform plasticity.

Hot Rolling

Roughing, intermediate and finishing stands progressively reduce the section into bars, rebars, sections or strip.

Quenching / TMT

Rebars pass through a controlled water-quench and self-tempering line, creating a hard outer layer and ductile core.

Cold Rolling & Forming

Pickled hot-rolled coils are cold-reduced for precise gauge and finish, or slit and formed into ERW pipes and hollow sections.

Testing & Dispatch

Products are cut to length, tested, tagged with heat numbers, bundled and dispatched with mill test certificates.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between TMT rebar and ordinary deformed bar?

TMT (thermo-mechanically treated) rebars are rapidly quenched after rolling and then self-tempered. This creates a hard, strong outer layer with a soft, ductile core — giving higher strength together with good bendability and weldability compared with conventional cold-twisted bars.

Do you supply mill test certificates?

Yes. Every bundle is tagged with its heat number, and mill test certificates showing chemical and mechanical results can be provided with your order.

Can you supply custom sizes or cut-to-length material?

Yes. We can supply non-standard lengths and many custom sizes depending on volume. Share your specification and our sales team will confirm feasibility and lead time.

Do you deliver outside Chattogram or export?

We deliver across Bangladesh and can support export enquiries. Our Chattogram location provides convenient access to the country's main seaport.
